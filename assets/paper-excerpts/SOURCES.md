# Paper excerpt source notes

The PNG files in this directory are unmodified 180 DPI page renders from the open Cordis paper, [A Programming Paradigm for Spatiotemporal Composability](https://github.com/cordiverse/paper/blob/main/paper.pdf), by Yifan Shi, Wei Zhang, and Tianyi Cui.

| Asset | Primary-source content | Article use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `cordis-paper-p29-29.png` | Page 29, Figure 1: base component lifecycle (`Inactive` and `Active`). | Establishes that a component has an explicit lifecycle. |
| `cordis-paper-p34-34.png` | Page 34, Figure 2: lifecycle with in-progress `Reloading` and `Unloading` states. | Establishes visible transition states and guarded withdrawal. |

The article's company-level claim is an inference: company skills should make their inputs, procedure, artifacts, review, authority, and stopping point explicit. The paper does not make that organisational claim.
